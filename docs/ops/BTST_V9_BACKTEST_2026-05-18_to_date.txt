NOTES / HONESTY
==========================================================================
(1) UNIVERSE = production ranker reconstruction. compute_top_n(con, signal_date,
    top_n=15, patterns=load_patterns(con)) on data/db/kanida_universe.db, the same
    function live_tier.run_cycle uses. Features (falcon_features) are the clean
    live week-to-date values and span the full window.
(2) CROSS-CHECK CAVEAT: for 07-08..07-24 the reconstructed top-15 only partially
    matches falcon_signals_live (rank-exact and set-overlap reported above). The
    gap is the promoted-pattern SNAPSHOT: load_patterns now returns 684 patterns,
    whereas the live 7.1.0 signals fired against a different (larger) promoted set,
    so n_fires/score and hence ranking differ. falcon_signals_live.fired_pattern_ids
    could give a bit-exact July reconstruction but does NOT exist pre-July, so the
    only universe reproducible across May-July is the current-snapshot compute_top_n.
    Treat the reconstructed universe as 'what today's engine would have picked on
    the clean historical features', NOT as a byte-replay of the live emission.
(3) signal_tier reconstructed via signal_tier.enrich_picks (production classifier /
    active rulebook). tier inputs use weekly-derived features flagged look-ahead
    elsewhere in memory; tier-conditioned numbers are indicative, not certified.
(4) ENTRY = 50:50 blend of the 09:15 and 09:16 one-minute bar OPENS from
    mkt_ohlc_1min (universe_engine db, reaches 07-23); fallback ohlc_1min then
    ohlc_daily open. Entry-source split: mkt_ohlc_1min 228, ohlc_1min 21, daily-open 7.
    CORRECTION TO THE ASSUMPTION: the directive expected only 07-24/25 to need the
    daily-open fallback, but 7 fully-closed trades on 07-13..07-23 also fell
    back because those specific symbols were absent from BOTH 1-min tables that day
    (listed above). They ARE in the P&L. Their entry = ohlc_daily open (not a 09:15/
    09:16 blend); impact is small (7 of 256 trades) but flagged for honesty. The
    07-24/25 entries never enter the P&L because their D2 close is not yet available.
(5) EXIT = D2 close (ohlc_daily). -6% disaster stop checked against min(D1 low,
    D2 low) from ohlc_daily (daily-low approximation; if it trips, fill at -6%).
(6) COST = 0.30% round-trip subtracted from gross per trade.
(7) PORTFOLIO = Rs 10L as 2 x Rs 5L sleeves alternating each traded day (2-session
    hold => each sleeve free again on its next turn); each sleeve redeploys its
    compounded capital equally across that day's high-tier names.
(8) BASELINE (-4.6% / 44%) is the raw BTST-Trader forward per operator directive;
    it is a Rs 5L single-sleeve forward log, so the return bases are not identical
    to V9's Rs 10L rolling-sleeve figure — win-rate and avg-net/trade are the
    cleaner apples-to-apples comparison.
