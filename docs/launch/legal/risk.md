your independent consideration only.** It is **NOT investment advice, NOT a

sell, or hold any security. It does not take into account your personal
financial situation, objectives, or risk appetite. **You alone decide what to do
with your money, and you are solely responsible for your trades.**

## 2. We are not a SEBI-registered intermediary

**Kanida.AI is not registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India
(SEBI) as an investment adviser, research analyst, stock broker, or portfolio
manager, and does not act in any such capacity.** We do not manage your money,
hold your funds or securities, or execute trades on your behalf. Any trades you
choose to place are executed by you through your own broker.

## 3. Past performance is not future results

**Past performance is not indicative of, and provides no guarantee of, future
results.** Markets change. A pattern that worked historically may not work
again. Different market regimes can and do break strategies that previously
performed well.

## 4. "Live", backtest, and simulated figures are MODEL track records

All performance figures shown on the Platform — including **win rates, hit
rates, backtests, walk-forward simulations, and the "Live" persona
portfolios** — are **model, simulated, or hypothetical track records derived
from historical and/or paper-traded data.** They are **NOT returns earned on
real customer capital and NOT a record of trades placed for you.** Hypothetical
and simulated results have inherent limitations: they are constructed with the
benefit of hindsight, may not reflect the impact of real-world trading costs,
slippage, liquidity, taxes, or your own behaviour, and **no representation is
made that any account will or is likely to achieve results similar to those
shown.** Headline figures (for example, long-run walk-forward growth numbers
shown on our credibility page) are illustrations of past, simulated engine
behaviour, not a forecast or promise.

## 5. No guarantee of returns; you can lose capital

**No assurance or guarantee of any return is made.** The value of investments
can go down as well as up. **You may lose some or all of your invested
capital.** Only invest money you can afford to lose. Leverage, derivatives, and
concentrated positions can increase losses beyond your initial outlay.

## 6. Market, data, and technology risks

Market data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. The Platform's models,
scores, and outputs may contain errors or fail in unforeseen market conditions.
Technical issues (outages, latency, bugs) may affect availability or content.
None of these reduce your responsibility for your own decisions.

## 7. Do your own research and seek advice

Before acting on any information from the Platform, consider doing your own
research and consulting a SEBI-registered investment adviser or other qualified
professional who can account for your personal circumstances.
