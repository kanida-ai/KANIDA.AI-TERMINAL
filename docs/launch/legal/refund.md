# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**DRAFT — pending legal review. Not yet legally binding.**

_Last updated: [DATE] · Effective: [DATE]_

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to paid subscriptions to the Kanida.AI
Platform operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Kanida.AI", "we", "us"). It should be
read together with our Terms of Service.

## 1. The plan

The Kanida.AI subscription is **₹999 per month (inclusive of applicable taxes
unless stated otherwise), billed monthly** and **auto-renewing** until you
cancel. Each payment buys access for one (1) monthly billing cycle. Payments are
processed in India by Razorpay.

## 2. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel at any time from your account billing page (or by contacting
support). When you cancel:

- **No further charges** are made after the current cycle.
- **Your access continues until the end of the billing cycle you have already
  paid for.** You are not cut off immediately.
- After that date, paid features are disabled until you resubscribe.

## 3. Refunds

Because access is delivered immediately and continuously for the full cycle you
have paid for, **we do not provide pro-rata refunds for the unused portion of a
billing cycle** once that cycle has started. Cancelling stops the next renewal;
it does not refund the current month.

We may, at our discretion, issue a refund in cases of: a duplicate or erroneous
charge; a charge after a valid cancellation; or a documented billing error on
our side. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via
Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days (timing depends on your bank or
payment provider).

## 4. Free (founding / comp) accounts

Founding members and invite-based complimentary ("comp") accounts are **free**.
There are no charges on these accounts and therefore nothing to refund.

## 5. Failed or missed payments

If an auto-renewal payment fails, we may retry the charge and/or notify you. If
payment is not completed, your subscription may lapse and paid access may be
suspended until payment succeeds. We do not charge you for a cycle you did not
receive access to.

## 6. Disputes and chargebacks

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us first at [SUPPORT
EMAIL] so we can resolve it quickly. Raising a chargeback with your bank without
contacting us may delay resolution and may result in suspension of your account
pending investigation.

## 7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy on prospective notice. Changes do not retroactively
affect a cycle already paid for.

## 8. Contact

Refund or billing questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
