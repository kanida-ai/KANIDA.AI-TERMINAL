## Benchmark: Instagram (elevate to this bar)
Pathfinder's research surface is a **full-screen, vertically-swipeable story/reel feed**, not a scrolling
list of cards:
- **One finding per screen**, immersive, 5–10 second read, thumb-swipe **up** for the next discovery,
  **swipe deeper** (tap/side) into a finding for evidence, related stocks, experiment history, follow-ups.
- **Clarity-first:** the first 2–3 stories deliver "what matters now"; the rest is endless optional depth.
- **Motion + polish to Instagram's bar:** smooth transitions, progress dots, tasteful micro-animation,
  premium typography — addictive but calm, never a quant terminal.
- **Honest-attractive:** the hook is curiosity + proof + the verdict scoreboard, never fake returns.

Keep KANIDA's dark + violet-Pathfinder identity; raise the *craft and interaction model* to Instagram-grade.
