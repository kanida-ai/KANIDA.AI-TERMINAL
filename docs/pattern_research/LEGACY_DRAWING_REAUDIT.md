# Original ten drawing re-audit

All 13 registered direction variants of CH01–CH10 were visually inspected and rechecked after presentation corrections. Their frozen source geometry passed independent coordinate, extrema and fitted-curve checks. This covers one actual occurrence per variant, not every historical formation or timeframe.

## Corrections

- Cup & Handle now uses the detector's actual fitted curve. The browser previously replaced it with a different curve based on candle lows.
- Horizontal Breakout and Flag & Pole no longer add swing points inferred separately in the browser.
- Both legacy names and catalogue IDs keep historic boundaries within the selected event.
- Dashed intersections are explicitly identified as geometric projections. Forming and confirmed states are visible.
